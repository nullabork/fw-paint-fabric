# FW Paint v3.1.0

**Gradients that follow a line, a gradient look for lines, and gravity.**

Targets: Minecraft **26.3** · Fabric Loader **0.19.5+** · Fabric API for 26.3 (Fabric jar
only) · Java **25**.

Your palettes, patterns, markers and settings carry over.

## New

- **Show as gradient.** On a line's Rendering page (the eye), next to Show as block: the line is
  drawn in the blocks of your active gradient palette, live, the way a Gradient fill would
  colour it. It stays on until you turn it off or reset the line's look.
  - It starts slightly see-through; the face opacity buttons change that, and 100% draws solid
    blocks.
  - Automatic segments have no block until you paint, so they show as a flat colour.
- **Gradient angle.** Also on the Rendering page: a pink plane cuts across the line with a ring
  and two grips, **T** (top) and **B** (bottom). Drag either round the ring to choose which way
  the Across gradient runs. Hold the Modifier (default **L-Ctrl**) for 15° steps.
  - The angle is saved with the line and turns with it when you rotate the line.
  - The tool goes away when you paint or click anything else.
- **Gradient options while painting.** In a painting mode with Gradient picked, look at a line
  and its cog appears. It opens the four gradient directions and the Rendering page, without
  switching to Line mode.
- **Gravity.** A line's toolbar has a new **Physics** button (a weight). Turn on Gravity and the
  line falls as one solid piece, hits the ground, tumbles and settles where something of that
  shape would come to rest, like a branch dropped on a hill. Use it to find a natural resting
  place before you paint; it moves the marker only and places nothing.
  - A line that is underground pops up first.
  - Press Gravity again to stop it mid-fall, or grab the line to catch it.
  - **Undo drop** puts the line back where it was.

## Changed

- **Across gradients follow the line.** Across used to run from the side you were looking at to
  the far side of the whole tube. Now the tube is cut into slices at right angles to the line,
  and in every slice the gradient runs from the bottom to the top. It follows every bend, a thin
  stretch gets the whole gradient like a thick one, and hollow tubes colour the same as solid
  ones. Where you stand no longer matters. Lines you already have paint this way too.
- The line toolbar now has three rows: shape, gradient direction, and rendering / physics /
  delete / help.

## Known limits

- Show as gradient, Gradient angle and Gravity are for lines only; boxes and rings do not have
  them yet.
- Show as gradient leaves out the palette's chaos, variation and step wobble, so the painted
  result can differ a little. Tubes over 60,000 blocks keep their plain look.
- Gravity treats every tube as round, so square and star tubes come to rest as if they were
  circular. It collides with blocks that have a collision shape (not water or plants).
- After a drop the line's nodes are off the block grid, as after a rotate. Undo drop is
  forgotten when you leave the world.
- A line that would fall out of the world, or over chunks that are not loaded, is put back.
