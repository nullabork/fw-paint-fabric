# FW Paint v3.0.0

**A new screen, a new wheel, one way to edit every marker — and markers you can restyle.**

Targets: Minecraft **26.3** · Fabric Loader **0.19.5+** · Fabric API for 26.3 (Fabric jar
only) · Java **25**.

Your palettes, patterns, markers and settings carry over. Old Circle and Square markers load as
Rings, and old lines keep their end sizes and twists.

## New

- **A redesigned screen.** Five tabs: **Paint · Place · Finder · Settings · Help**.
  - **Paint**: a **Paint with** switch (Solid / Gradient / Noise / Pattern) over that paint's
    library — your palettes or patterns with **Use / Edit / Duplicate / Delete**, previews and a
    summary, or for Solid the block list and the four **Which block paints** modes.
  - **Place**: the modes as tiles, grouped **Blocks / Markers / Shapes** like the wheel, with
    the selected mode's options beside them (greyed where they don't apply) and the marker
    settings.
  - **Settings**: click a row to set the paint tool; colour match, missing blocks, paint
    memory, Move HUD, and a list of every key and gesture as currently bound.
  - Every section has one **?** that opens its Help page. The open key also closes the screen.
- **Redesigned palette and pattern editors.** Both have their own top bar (‹ Paint, name,
  **● unsaved changes**, Cancel, Save).
  - Palette: columns in working order — **1 Pick blocks · 2 Arrange · 3 How it blends** — with
    labelled switches for Order, Curve, Sizing and Variation, a foldable Noise section, and one
    preview (**As gradient | As noise**).
  - Pattern: **Ink · Canvas · Preview**, with a tool bar (**Draw · Line · Fill · Pick ·
    Origin**) that chooses what plain left-click does; the old shortcuts still work from any
    tool.
- **A redesigned wheel.** The first ring is **Paint · Palette · Place**, in tab order. Place fans
  out **Blocks**, **Markers**, **Shapes**, a new **Options** ring (Don't match palette, Fill
  voids first, Place in air, Perp snap) and **Off**. Every ring ends in **More… (K)**, which
  opens the screen on that tab. Labels stay readable in small windows.
- **Recent bar** on the wheel: your last picks along the bottom, newest first, saved between
  sessions. Click one to set it again; it switches the paint type too if it needs to.
- **Marker rendering.** The new **eye** button on a box, ring or line toolbar opens a Rendering
  page for that marker:
  - **Colour** its faces and its edge lines separately (eight colours each; click the lit one
    for the default).
  - **Opacity** of faces and of edges, in 10% steps.
  - **Show as block**: pick any block from a filterable list and the marker is drawn with that
    block's textures. It is a preview only — nothing is placed.
  - **Show when obscured**: choose whether faces and edge lines still show where real blocks
    are in front of them (edges do by default, faces don't).
  - The look is saved with the marker. **Reset** puts it back.
- **Only place inside markers** (Place tab, off by default): a click on the outside of a box,
  ring, tube or start-to-end run places nothing, so a slip can't spread a blob over the outside.
  The HUD shows a yellow reminder while it's on.
- **One toolbar per marker.** Every Box, Ring, Line and block-marker group has a grey **cog**:
  left-click it for a floating toolbar with that marker's actions, Delete and **?**. The line's
  two end toolbars are gone. Controls are coloured by what dragging does — Start teal, End
  orange, Move white, Size green, Turn blue, Bend pink.
- **The box is editable.** Drag the green face handles to resize, the white cube to move,
  scroll to move, Ctrl+scroll to resize, right-click a face to grow it by one (Ctrl: shrink).
  The toolbar picks the gradient axis (X / Y / Z) and Flip.
- **Size and twist on every line node**, eased between nodes, so tubes can bulge and pinch.
  Drag a node's green size or blue twist cube (Ctrl: all nodes; right-click resets);
  Ctrl+scroll on a node resizes it.
- **Block-marker groups**: touching starts on one plane, with their ends, get a cog toolbar —
  Re-place ends, Flip, Delete ends, Delete group.
- **Line length on the HUD**: with a line marker in view the HUD shows its length and node
  count.
- **B** now also steps through Solid's blocks, not just palettes and patterns.

## Changed

- **Circle and Square are one Ring mode.** New rings start round; switch **Circle / Square** on
  the toolbar. Rings also gain **Flip**, which starts an extruded ring's gradient from the far
  end.
- **One Modifier key** (default **L-Ctrl**) replaces the separate clear-connected and link keys.
  It means "the other way / both ends / all nodes" everywhere. If you had rebound either old
  key, rebind Modifier under Controls → FW Paint.
- **Middle-click deletes the smallest piece you aim at** (one block marker, a line's middle
  node, otherwise the whole marker); **Ctrl+middle-click** deletes the whole group or line. This
  replaces Ctrl+left-click to clear connected markers.
- **The HUD** reads "Paint · Mode" on line one, and shows yellow lines only for options that
  apply and aren't at their default. It has a dark backing so it stays readable over bright
  scenes. Move HUD previews your real HUD.
- **Messages** all read "FW Paint · Area: what happened — K › Tab", naming your real open key.
- **Making a shape walks you through it** ("step 1 of 2 — …"), and aiming at a control names
  its keys on the action bar.
- The marker toolbar stays put while you look around, and draws on top of the marker's
  highlight.
- Help is rewritten for all of the above.

## Fixed

- Faint text and hints are brighter and easier to read across the screen and wheel.
- Middle-click on a block marker inside a box, ring or tube deletes the block marker, not the
  region around it.

## Known limits

- Start/end block-marker groups have no Rendering page yet.
- Show as block draws every block as a full cube; very large markers fall back to the plain
  tint.
- At small window sizes a few long labels and hints on the Place tab, Settings tab and editors
  are cut off at the edge.
