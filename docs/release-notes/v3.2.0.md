# FW Paint v3.2.0

**One Shape tool, drawn profiles for lines, and dig modes.**

Targets: Minecraft **26.3** · Fabric Loader **0.19.5+** · Fabric API for 26.3 (Fabric jar
only) · Java **25**.

Your palettes, patterns and settings carry over. Boxes and rings from earlier versions come back
as shapes; start and end block markers are gone (see Removed).

## New

- **Shape tool.** Box and Ring are now one tool, **Shape**, and it works like the line tool.
  Left-click one corner, left-click the opposite corner, and you get a box. Its cog toolbar
  turns it into a **Cylinder, Triangle, Star or Sphere**, **Hollow or Filled**, and a hollow
  one **Capped** or open like a pipe.
  - Drag the bottom and top faces for height, and the two green side cubes for width and
    depth. Dragging one side makes an oval or a slab; hold the Modifier (default **L-Ctrl**) to
    keep the proportions. A hollow shape has a third cube for wall thickness. Sizes land on
    whole blocks.
  - It has the line's move cube, rotate rings, gradient directions, Gradient angle, Show as
    gradient, Rendering page and Physics. Gradients run bottom face to top face by default.
- **Drawn profile for lines.** A new profile button on the line toolbar sweeps a shape you draw
  yourself. Draw it block by block, flat against any face: left-drag draws, right-drag rubs out,
  and the pencil reopens it. The drawing stays where you drew it when the line moves, and each
  node's size scales it.
  - A line running through the drawing sweeps it exactly as drawn. A line running along it
    stands the drawing up, with the side furthest from you on top.
- **Dig modes.** A pick button on every marker's toolbar steps **Off → Dig inside → Dig
  outside**.
  - Dig inside: while part of the marker is within your reach, only blocks inside it break, so
    you mine out exactly its shape. Out of reach, you dig as normal.
  - Dig outside: blocks inside the marker can't be broken, so you can clear everything round it
    and leave its shape standing.
  - It works with any tool, in survival and creative, and is saved with the marker. The action
    bar says which marker stopped you.

## Changed

- **Markers menu.** Place now has two groups: Blocks, and Markers (**Shape**, **Line / Spline**,
  Off). Line is renamed Line / Spline. Shapes and lines can be edited in either marker mode; the
  mode only decides what a click on empty space makes.
- **Controls stay on your side.** The move, cog and rotate cubes of a line or shape now sit just
  off its surface at the spot nearest you and follow as you walk, so they are never underneath
  it or under the ground. The toolbar opens beside the cog.
- **Fill voids first** now applies to face fills inside a shape or tube.
- Remembered wheel picks for Box, Ring, Marker or Marker draw select Shape.

## Fixed

- A hollow tube on a slope showed stray columns of blocks through its middle. The tube builder
  could skip cells at some angles; it no longer can.
- Moving a marker with the axis lock (default **Alt**) held drifted off the axis.

## Removed

- **Marker and Marker draw** (the start and end block markers) and the Auto end marker setting.
  Markers placed with them are no longer shown or used; their save file is left on disk.
- Boxes and rings convert to shapes when a world loads. Once that world saves, older versions
  of the mod won't see them.

## Known limits

- Dig modes stop only your own breaking by hand, not explosions, pistons or other players. For
  a hollow shape or a shell tube, "inside" is only its walls. On NeoForge the dig hook is
  untested in play.
- Gravity treats a shape as a chain of balls along its axis, so a wide flat shape settles as if
  it were round.
- A drawn profile gets blocky when a node is scaled up, and fine detail can smear on tight
  bends. When the line runs exactly along the drawer's left-right, it reads the right way round
  looking from the line's start; Flip swaps it.
- A converted square ring that was rotated may come back turned the other way.
