# FW Paint v3.0.1

**Turn a whole line, and line controls that stay in reach.**

Targets: Minecraft **26.3** · Fabric Loader **0.19.5+** · Fabric API for 26.3 (Fabric jar
only) · Java **25**.

Your palettes, patterns, markers and settings carry over.

## New

- **Rotate a line.** A blue **rotate** cube (globe icon) sits beside a line's move cube and cog.
  Left-click it and two rings go round the whole line — green and red, one per axis. A ring
  turns white when you aim at it; grab it anywhere and pull to turn the line about its centre.
  - The tube's shape, every node's size and twist, and the other ring all turn with it.
  - The action bar shows the angle as you pull. Hold the Modifier (default **L-Ctrl**) for 15°
    steps.
  - The rings stay relative to the line and are saved with it. Left-click the cube again to put
    them away.

## Changed

- **Line controls stay in reach.** The move cube, cog and rotate cube now ride together and
  slide along the line to stay a few blocks from where you stand, so on a long line you no
  longer walk back to the middle for them. They line up side by side across your view, so one
  never hides another. Both follow where you stand, not where you look, so they hold still
  under the crosshair.
- **Hover help in the palette and pattern editors** describes the option under the mouse — not
  the one that is switched on — and says when a click would switch to it.

## Fixed

- The action-bar hint for a marker control no longer fades while you are still aiming at that
  control. It goes once you look away.
- CurseForge uploads no longer fail when the release notes contain a semicolon.

## Known limits

- The rotate rings are sized to the whole line, so on a long line they are large and can pass
  close to you.
- Nodes are not snapped back to the block grid after a turn (the shape is kept exactly);
  dragging a node afterwards snaps that node as usual.
- Start/end block-marker groups have no Rendering page yet.
- Show as block draws every block as a full cube; very large markers fall back to the plain
  tint.
- At small window sizes a few long labels and hints on the Place tab, Settings tab and editors
  are cut off at the edge.
